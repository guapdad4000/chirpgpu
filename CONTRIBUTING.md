# Contributing

Good contributions:
- A prompt or routine you actually used, with what it did for you.
- A fix to something that's wrong or out of date (platform fees change; rules change).
- Your own ledger results in `community/` (optional). Show costs, not just revenue.

Not accepted:
- Income claims, "make $X" framing, or referral links without disclosure.
- Anything that automates spam, fake reviews, or breaks a platform's rules.

How: open an issue first for anything big. Small fixes, just send a PR. Keep the voice plain.

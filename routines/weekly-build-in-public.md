# Routine: weekly build-in-public post

**When:** every Sunday, 5:00 PM Pacific
**Owner time:** ~10 minutes (review and approve)

## Steps
1. Read the week's ledger rows and daily reports.
2. Fill in `templates/weekly-update.md`.
3. Draft one X thread (4–6 posts). No newsletter yet.
4. Include: money in, money out, balance, owner hours, what broke, what's next.
5. Queue for approval. Post only after the owner says yes.

## Tone check before posting
- [ ] Every number is in the ledger.
- [ ] Costs are shown next to revenue.
- [ ] No promises about anyone else's results.
- [ ] At least one honest "this didn't work."
